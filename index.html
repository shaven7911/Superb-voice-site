The problem

Missed calls become someone else’s jobs.

When a furnace quits at 9 p.m., a pipe bursts on a Sunday, or a storm opens up a roof leak, people call the first company that answers. If your phone goes to voicemail, many callers won’t leave a message. They call the next name on the list.

The same thing happens during the day when your office is slammed: the line is busy, the call rolls over, and the work goes to a competitor.

One missed emergency job can cost more than a month of Superb Voice.

Where calls slip through

  • After hours. Evenings, weekends, and holidays, when emergencies tend to happen.
  • Busy during the day. Your office is on another line, helping a customer at the counter, or out to lunch. Instead of ringing out, the call rolls over to Superb Voice.
  • Rush periods. Storms, heat waves, and seasonal rushes when every line is lit up.
  • Voicemail. Callers with urgent problems rarely wait for a callback.
  • Thin messages. A hang-up or a short voicemail often leaves out the email and what the job actually is.
How it works

Up and running in three steps

Your team keeps answering during the day. Superb Voice picks up when they can’t.

  1. Forward the calls you miss

    Set your phone to forward after-hours and unanswered or busy calls to your Superb Voice line. Your main number stays the same.

  2. We answer as your company

    The agent greets callers with your business name and collects their name, phone, email, and job details.

  3. You get a summary by email

    We email your business what the caller needs so you can call them back. Urgent calls are marked in that summary.

FAQ

Common questions

Does my front desk still answer?

Yes. Superb Voice is backup for the front desk. Your people answer during business hours. It covers after-hours calls and the overflow they can’t get to.

What happens on an urgent call?

It collects the caller’s name, phone, email, and what happened, marks the call urgent, and emails you the summary so you can call back. Texting your on-call tech is optional and set up for your business. It isn’t an emergency service.

How is pricing different?

No per-call or per-minute fees, so a busy storm week doesn’t raise your bill. Setup starts at $250, monthly pricing is set with you, and every client starts with a free pilot.

Do I have to switch phone companies?

No. Superb Voice works with your current carrier. You keep your number and forward the calls you miss.

Do calls get recorded?

We follow your preference. If you choose to record calls, callers are told the call may be recorded. Recordings and transcripts are used to write the summary we email you and, when you have turned them on, to book an appointment or text your on-call tech.

Which tools do you work with?

Works alongside FieldPulse and similar field-service tools, plus Gmail and Outlook. Optional, set up for your business: appointment booking, text alerts to your on-call tech, and Google or Outlook Calendar sync. See integrations.

Can I hear it before I commit?

Yes. Call the demo line at (409) 377-6497. Play the customer and see how it handles your kind of call.

It’s an AI. It can mishear, it isn’t an emergency service, and it doesn’t replace your staff. It catches the calls they can’t.

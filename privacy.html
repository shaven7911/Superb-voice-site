Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Superb Voice (“we,” “us”) is a d/b/a of Superb Environmental LLC. We provide an AI voice agent that answers after-hours and overflow phone calls for home-service and trades businesses (“clients”). This policy explains, in plain language, what information we handle and why.

Who this applies to

  • Callers: people who call a client’s phone line and reach Superb Voice, or who call our demo line.
  • Clients: the businesses that use Superb Voice.
  • Website visitors: people who visit superb-voice.com or email us.

What we collect

  • Call information: the phone number you call from, the date, time, and length of the call, and what you tell the agent (for example your name, phone, email, and the job details).
  • Recordings and transcripts: calls may be recorded and transcribed so we can deliver the service. When a client has chosen to record calls, callers are told the call may be recorded.
  • Client account information: business contact details and the settings needed to run the service (such as greetings and, when enabled, appointment booking, on-call text alerts, and calendar sync).
  • Emails you send us: whatever you include in your message.

This website has no sign-up form, no account system, and no advertising trackers. The contact form only opens your own email app.

How we use it

  • To answer calls, collect the caller’s name, phone, email, and job details, and email that summary to the client so they can call back.
  • When the client has enabled them, to book appointments, send text alerts to the client’s on-call tech, or add visits to Google or Outlook Calendar.
  • To review and improve call quality and troubleshoot problems.
  • To respond to emails and support our clients.

We do not sell personal information.

Who we share it with

  • The client you called. Call details, transcripts, and (if enabled) recordings are shared with the business you were trying to reach.
  • The client’s email. Call summaries are emailed to the client (for example Gmail or Outlook).
  • Optional booking, calendar, and text alerts. If the client enables them, appointments may be booked, visits may be added to Google or Outlook Calendar, and urgent calls may trigger text-message alerts to the client’s on-call tech. Those providers’ own privacy policies apply to data they receive.
  • Service providers. We use third-party providers for things like phone service, speech recognition, AI processing, messaging, and hosting. They process data only to help us provide the service.
  • Legal reasons. If required by law, or to protect someone’s safety or our legal rights.

How long we keep it

We keep call information only as long as needed to provide the service to the client, or as the client directs, and as required by law. Information the client stores in their own email, calendar, or other tools is kept according to that client’s own practices.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no system is perfectly secure.

Your choices

If you called one of our clients, you can contact that business directly about your information. You can also email us to ask about, correct, or delete information we hold, and we’ll help or point you to the right business.

Children

The service is meant for business use and isn’t directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

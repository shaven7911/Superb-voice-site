The service

Superb Voice is an AI voice agent that backs up the front desk for home-service and trades businesses. It answers after-hours and overflow calls, collects the caller’s name, phone, email, and job details, and emails the client a summary so they can call back. Optional features, set up for a business when that client enables them, include appointment booking, text alerts to an on-call tech, and Google or Outlook Calendar sync.

AI limitations
